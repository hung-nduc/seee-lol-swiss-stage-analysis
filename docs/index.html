Method

  • Results come from the 33 match records (PDF). The Excel schedule is the final authority for teams and sides.
  • Game stats were transcribed from the 28 post-game scoreboards. Each team's kills, deaths, assists and gold were checked against the totals in the scoreboard header.
  • Ranks are an op.gg (VN) snapshot from 22 Nov 2026 for season 2026: current tier, LP, and wins + losses per queue. They are not pre-tournament ranks.
  • Team rank = average of the players who actually played, weighted by Swiss games. Ladder: Iron 4 = 0 … Gold 4 = 12 … Diamond 1 = 27, Master = 28 + LP/100.
  • Upset = a played game won by the lineup with the lower average Solo/Duo rank.
