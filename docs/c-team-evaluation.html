Kill participation (KP)

KP is the share of a team's kills that a player took part in (kills + assists ÷ team kills). Team KP is the average of its five players' KP in a game, averaged over the team's games. A high value means fights are played together, with kills shared across the map. A low value means kills come from isolated plays, or that one or two players do most of the killing. Read it next to kills per game: a team that gets very few kills (MSD, 5 per game) shows a high KP simply because every kill is a group effort.
