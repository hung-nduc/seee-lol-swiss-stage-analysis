B. Swiss Stage evaluation

33 matches over 5 rounds (16–22 Nov 2026): 28 played, 4 forfeits, 1 ruling. Results against rank, surprises, ban & pick, meta and counter-picks on patches 26.22/26.23, and which position carries the damage.

Key findings

  • The higher-ranked lineup won 26 of 28 played games; only 2 went against rank. 5 matches were decided without a game (4 no-shows, 1 ruling).
  • Most surprising result: LS53 – the 2-2 elimination decider: LHP (Platinum 3) beat ZEN (Emerald 4) in 37:40 and knocked out the 5th-ranked team. The largest rank gap overcome was LS36 (YSL over CBK, 4.6 divisions), but YSL's average rests on only 3 ranked players.
  • Seeding by rank vs. result: ZEN (rank #5) missed the quarter-finals; LHP (rank #9) made it.
  • Side: blue won 14 of 28 played games (50%), red 14 (50%).
  • Games lasted 29.9 min on average (median 29.8); fastest LS11 21:30, longest LS33 40:44.
  • 103 different champions were picked and 89 banned across 28 games; 34 champions were picked only once. Most contested (appearance = picked or banned): Kai'Sa 68%, Jarvan IV 64%, Nocturne 54%, Nautilus 50%, Ornn 43% of games.
  • Damage is carried by Top and Mid (25.3% and 25.3% of team damage). Top laners were the team's #1 damage dealer 18 times, Mid 17 times. The ADC takes the most gold (23.5%) but deals 22.7% of damage.
  • Meta: 56% of picks were op.gg tier 1–2 for their patch (26.22 in round 1, 26.23 after). Most meta-forward: YSL (72%); least: LK (30%). Meta picks won 49% and counter-picks into a favourable solo-queue matchup won only 33% (14/43): at this level the player gap mattered more than the draft.
