Key findings

  • TT fields the strongest Solo/Duo lineup (Master 231 LP), followed by DMX (Master 43 LP), LXM (Master 33 LP). The gap from #3 to #4 (GOS, Emerald 3) is about 7 divisions.
  • 7 of the 8 quarter-finalists were also among the 8 highest-ranked lineups.
  • Ranked 5s (tournament mode) is barely used: only 5 of 90 players have a 5s rank (lil moneytft#momly – DMX, HK4 Cragon#0608 – HKG, HK3 XII#1268 – HKG, SSA Reaper#2907 – LK, W1ndchaser#1845 – LN).
  • Teams leaning on Flex: ZEN (56% of ranked games), VNT (51% of ranked games). The top teams mostly grind Solo/Duo: KGC 6%, DMX 9%, GOS 16%, TT 18%.
  • Widest skill gaps inside a team: GLT (25 divisions, from LSR Nhakanasi#TLA4 (Master 378 LP) to nhquan2121#6844 (Bronze 2)), FTL (20 divisions, from dang khoa#1701 (Diamond 4) to Tbbbbb#09876 (Bronze 3)), LN (18 divisions, from hadyu#9816 (Master 214 LP) to 30FPS player#2007 (Silver 1)).
  • Teams with players who have no 2026 Solo/Duo rank (their average leans on fewer players): LN (1), GLT (1), YSL (4).
